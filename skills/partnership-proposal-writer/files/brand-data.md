When listing collaboration options for a partner, draw from this list — but only include what actually makes sense for that partner:

- **Dedicated tutorial or use-case video** — YouTube video featuring the product in a real workflow
- **Course integration** — Adding the tool into existing AI/automation courses (reaches existing students)
- **Facebook community posts/promotions** — Posts, pinned content, or discussions in the 330k-member communities
- **Email campaign** — Dedicated send or feature in a newsletter to the 10k+ list
- **LinkedIn content** — Post or article for professional/B2B reach

## What to Extract from Inbound Inquiries

When a company reaches out, look for:
- Their product name and what it does
- Their claimed user/customer base (useful to acknowledge)
- Any specific ask they made
- Their tone (formal vs casual) — mirror it slightly

Then write back with the right channel mix for their product, and position Krystian as someone who has real reach and wants a structured deal — not someone who does cheap shoutouts.

## Notes on Tailoring

- **AI tools, creative tools, productivity apps** → Facebook communities + YouTube + paid students are strong fits
- **B2B / professional tools** → Lead with LinkedIn, email list, and note the entrepreneur/founder segment
- **E-learning or course platforms** → Lead with Udemy integration angle (55k existing students)
- **Consumer apps with mass appeal** → Lead with Facebook (330k) as the headline number
- If a partner mentions specific interest in video content → Feature YouTube integration prominently
- Never pad with channels that don't fit just to make the list longer — it weakens credibility
